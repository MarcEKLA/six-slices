How to play

Your quest: 6 pieces, 6 digital jobs.

The goal

Win all 6 slices, one for each digital job. Then go back to the center of the board and answer the final question.

A turn

First, roll the die and move your piece. Then look at the square you land on:

  • Colored square: answer a question from that category.
  • HQ: do a mission in 30 seconds.
  • Hack Alert!: a hacker attacks. Be careful!
  • Roll again: lucky you! Roll the die one more time.

The 3 levels

Level Help Reward
Newbie Multiple choice (QCM) +1 XP
Junior With a hint +2 XP
Expert No help +3 XP

XP

With 3 XP, you can choose one power:

  • Roll the die again.
  • Change your question.
  • Block the hacker.

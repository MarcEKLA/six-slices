How to play

Your quest: 6 pieces, 6 digital jobs.

The goal

Win the 6 pieces, one for each digital job.

Then go back to the center and answer the final question.

A turn

First, roll the die. Then look at your square:

  • Color square: answer a question from this category.
  • HQ: do a mission in 30 seconds.
  • Hack Alert! A hacker attacks you. Be careful!
  • Roll again: lucky you! Roll the die one more time.

XP

With 3 XP, you can:

  • roll the die again,
  • change your question,
  • block the hacker.

Game Rules

Are you ready to cook?

2 to 6 players, 45 to 60 minutes, age 15+. English level: A2 - B1.

Goal

Fill your hexagon with the 6 quest pieces (one per color), then win the final question at the center of the board.

Components

  • One board, one die and one 30-second sand timer
  • 6 pawns and 6 player hexagons
  • 36 quest pieces (6 per color)
  • Question cards (3 decks), Mission cards and Hack Alert! cards
  • 6 Job cards
  • XP tokens

Setup

  • Each player places their cake-slice pawn at the center of the board, on the color of their large cake. Everyone starts with 0 XP.
  • Place the Hack Alert! cards and the Mission cards on the board.
  • The youngest player starts.

On your turn

Roll the die, move in any direction, then look at your square:

  • Color square: answer a question of that color. Right: earn XP and play again.
  • Headquarters: complete a Mission in 30 seconds. Success: win the quest piece and read the Job card aloud.
  • Hack Alert! "Safe" or "Scam"? Right: +2 XP. Wrong: the hacker steals one of your quest pieces.
  • Roll again: roll the die again.

Questions: 3 levels

Before the question, choose your level. Another player draws a card from that deck and reads you the question for your color. The answer is on the back.

LevelQuestionReward
EasyMultiple choice, 3 answers1 XP
MediumQuestion with a clue2 XP
HardQuestion with no help3 XP

Missions

A small challenge from the job: invent a slogan, write a post, put code in the right order...

The other players vote with a thumbs up, or you check the answer on the card.

XP

Each bonus costs 3 XP:

  • Roll the die again
  • Change your question
  • Block the hacker (antivirus)

Winning

With your 6 quest pieces, go to the center.

The other players choose the color of a Hard question. If you get it right, you win! If not, try again on your next turn.

To finish, everyone says which job they would like to try.

Reminder: the 6 colors

  • Marketing - Digital Marketer
  • Social Media - Community Manager
  • Development - Software Developer
  • Design - Graphic Designer
  • UX/UI - UX/UI Designer
  • Data & AI - Data Analyst
